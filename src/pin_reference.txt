Single Servo Wiring (3 wires)
=============================

  Yellow or Orange  →  D5 on ESP8266 (signal)
  Red               →  VIN on ESP8266 (motor power — ~5V when USB-powered)
  Brown             →  GND on ESP8266

  Do NOT connect servo red to 3.3V — not enough power for the motor.

  D5 (GPIO14) is used instead of D4 so uploads work with the servo connected.

  If the servo buzzes but barely moves, use a separate 5V supply:
    supply +5V  →  servo red AND ESP VIN
    supply GND  →  servo brown AND ESP GND  (grounds must be tied together)

Camera (FalconEye)  →  USB cable to your PC (not to ESP8266)

Only ONE servo = horizontal (left/right) movement.
The PC still calculates up/down in software; with one servo only left/right moves the motor.

HARDWARE TROUBLESHOOTING (servo won't move at all)
==================================================
  1. Upload src/servo_hardware_test/ (no WiFi) — if horn still does not move = hardware
  2. Confirm yellow wire is on D5 (not D4, not 3.3V)
  3. Red on VIN, brown on GND — if red/brown swapped, servo will not work
  4. USB power alone often fails — use phone charger 5V to VIN + shared GND
  5. Buzzing but no movement = weak power OR broken servo gear
  6. Totally silent + no movement = no power, wrong pin, or dead servo
  7. Try another servo or another USB cable/port if available
