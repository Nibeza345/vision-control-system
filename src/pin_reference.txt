Single Servo Wiring (3 wires)
=============================

  Yellow or Orange  →  D4 on ESP8266 (signal)
  Red               →  5V on ESP8266
  Brown             →  GND on ESP8266

Camera (FalconEye)  →  USB cable to your PC (not to ESP8266)

Only ONE servo = horizontal (left/right) movement.
The PC still calculates up/down in software; with one servo only left/right moves the motor.
